## 💫 About Me:

I'm a Software Developer and AI/ML Enthusiast with hands-on experience building intelligent systems, retrieval-augmented AI architectures, and full-stack web applications. Currently, I'm focused on developing NLP and Generative AI applications—such as multilingual RAG-powered assistants and medical chatbots—alongside computer vision pipelines for automated document identification.

I have a strong interest in combining machine learning models with scalable engineering to create impactful, real-world solutions. Whether it's environmental forecasting like Air Quality Index (AQI) prediction, healthcare assistants, or full-stack clinical appointment systems, I enjoy transforming complex problems into clean, usable software.

At the moment, I'm deepening my expertise in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, and system design to build end-to-end intelligent systems. I'm always open to collaborating on AI/ML projects, backend development, and open-source innovations.

Feel free to explore my repositories to see my work, and reach out if you'd like to collaborate, discuss ideas, or connect. I'm always excited to work on meaningful projects and grow as a developer.
